IMPORTANT VALIDATION NOTE
==========================
value_in_use = 1 / an object existing / a feature being enabled is NOT by itself proof of a vulnerability.
For every result, validate:
1) Who can use the feature/object;
2) Whether the tested principal is actually low-privileged;
3) Whether the relevant permission is reachable by that principal;
4) What security boundary is crossed (database, server, OS, remote server, sensitive data);
5) Whether the behavior is required/approved by the client baseline.

Use the "VALIDATION" blocks added below each applicable command before assigning a finding or severity.
Do not execute OS commands, modify jobs, impersonate privileged accounts, or access/exfiltrate sensitive data solely to prove impact unless the engagement explicitly authorizes that validation.
